# Mini PC AI Lab — evaluation hardware proposal

**Testing affordable hardware for real-world local AI development.**

Project: [github.com/McCullochLabs/mini-pc-ai-lab](https://github.com/McCullochLabs/mini-pc-ai-lab).

Mini PC AI Lab is a new independent initiative developing reproducible evaluations of affordable computers for local AI development. It is not an established hardware publication. There are no audience, sponsorship, or measured performance claims in this proposal.

The initiative draws on inspected Android build and automated-test artifacts and agent workflow source; retained evidence is distinct from tests rerun by this lab. Development activities motivate questions about coding, testing/review and structured automation. The accompanying [technical portfolio](../docs/TECHNICAL_PORTFOLIO.md) and [evidence register](../docs/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md) distinguish inspected evidence, owner reports and proposed work. Private commercial code and assets are excluded. These activities do not establish prior hardware-review experience.

**Request.** One complimentary evaluation unit, refurbished/open-box machine, donated retired workstation, or temporary loaner. A previous-generation mini PC with 32–64 GB RAM and SSD storage is useful; an expandable desktop or GPU workstation would answer a separate research question. A compatible complete system is preferred to avoid unbudgeted components. No equipment or terms have been accepted.

**Proposed evaluation.** Record exact configuration, OS/software versions, model identity and quantization. Test small coding models, deterministic synthetic coding/repair tasks, and one/two/four simultaneous requests to one model. Report task success separately from throughput, latency and memory use. GPU support is verified on the actual runtime; NPU marketing figures are not substituted for inference measurements. Power and thermal findings are included only where measured with suitable tools. Extended workloads require owner approval.

**Useful deliverables.** A configuration/setup note, reproducible commands and synthetic fixtures, machine-readable measurements, a concise technical report with failures and limits, and a hardware-support disclosure. Reports are intended for this public GitHub repository after technical review; no reach, schedule or favorable outcome is guaranteed. A feasible scope and target timetable will be agreed after hardware and access are confirmed.

**Independence.** All donations, loans and financial relationships will be disclosed. Partners may correct factual errors but cannot approve conclusions, suppress negative findings or purchase favorable results. No proprietary assets or confidential correspondence will be published without authorization.

**Practical arrangements.** Donation and loaner options are welcome. For a loaner, written ownership, duration, permitted tests/upgrades, return shipping, insurance, damage responsibility and data-erasure terms must be reviewed by the owner before acceptance. A discount is not treated as free equipment and creates no purchase commitment.

**Next step.** Please indicate whether an appropriate evaluation contact and unit might be available. The test plan and editorial policy can be shared for scope discussion. Initial non-confidential inquiries can be opened as GitHub issues; keep personal contact, shipping and agreement details private.
