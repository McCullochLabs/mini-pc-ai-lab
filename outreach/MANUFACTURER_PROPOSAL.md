# Manufacturer evaluation proposal

McCullochLabs AI Hardware Lab is an independent development initiative evaluating accessible hardware for local AI development. We welcome a prior-generation or current evaluation unit on an agreed loan, with a complete system preferred. Suitable studies include CPU-only baselines, accelerated inference and development work alongside model serving.

An evaluation plan names the hardware configuration, available workloads and limits before testing. Reports distinguish request latency, throughput, correctness and overall workflow completion. They include missing telemetry, failures and configuration caveats. See the [benchmark methodology](../BENCHMARK_METHODOLOGY.md).

We disclose all support and retain control of methods and conclusions. A partner may suggest factual corrections. Coverage, favorable findings and audience reach are never guaranteed. McCullochLabs has no registered nonprofit status.

Before dispatch, agree in writing on authorized custody, sanitization, included accessories, permitted modifications, shipping and insurance, return date and costs. See the [partnership terms](../PARTNERSHIPS.md) and [reusable inquiry](MANUFACTURER_LOANER.md).
