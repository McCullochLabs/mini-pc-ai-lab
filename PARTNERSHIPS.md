# Hardware partnerships

McCullochLabs AI Hardware Lab welcomes evaluation loaners, donated retired equipment, refurbished systems and compatible components. We are an independent development initiative and have no registered nonprofit status. Our current role is early-stage development research focused on reproducible local AI workloads. We offer no charitable eligibility, guaranteed promotion or favorable coverage.

We welcome manufacturers and evaluation teams, corporate IT and retiring businesses, IT asset disposition providers, recyclers, refurbishers and component suppliers. A working prior-generation system can be useful. See the [hardware priorities](docs/HARDWARE_WISHLIST.md) and [outreach materials](outreach/README.md).

## Before hardware ships

- Confirm the sender has authority to transfer or lend the hardware. Record whether title transfers or remains with the lender, the asset and included parts, condition, any restrictions and the disposition of failed hardware.
- Have the sender remove storage or sanitize it under its own policy and remove enrollment, firmware and account locks. Agree in writing on any return sanitization or media removal. Keep asset tags, serials, shipping information and agreements private.
- Agree on packing, shipping, insurance, responsibility for damage, evaluation period, allowed upgrades, return deadline, return label and costs. Get written agreement for extensions or any change in ownership.
- Disclose the source and nature of all hardware or financial support with the evaluation. Partners may offer factual corrections. McCullochLabs controls methods, scoring, publication and conclusions, including unfavorable results. See the [editorial policy](EDITORIAL_POLICY.md).

Start with a nonconfidential GitHub issue if appropriate. Exchange contact, ownership and shipping details privately once both parties agree to proceed.
