# Hardware partnerships

Mini PC AI Lab welcomes evaluation units, loaners, donated systems, refurbished/open-box computers and appropriate components for independent local AI evaluation. The initiative is new and does not promise audience reach, positive coverage or results before testing.

The [one-page proposal](outreach/MANUFACTURER_PROPOSAL.md), [hardware wishlist](docs/HARDWARE_WISHLIST.md), [portfolio](docs/TECHNICAL_PORTFOLIO.md) and [methodology](BENCHMARK_METHODOLOGY.md) explain the proposed value. A complete working previous-generation mini PC with 32–64 GB RAM and SSD, or an expandable retired workstation, is a useful starting point. Complete-system cost and compatible runtime matter more than processor marketing figures.

For an initial non-confidential inquiry, open an issue in this repository. Do not post personal contact information, shipping addresses, private correspondence or agreement terms publicly. No existing hardware partner or accepted equipment is claimed.

Before acceptance, the owner must review ownership, duration, permitted testing/upgrades, warranty, support, shipping/return costs, insurance, damage responsibility, financial ties and data-erasure obligations. Record equipment identifiers privately. Use original synthetic workloads and confirm lawful transfer/data sanitization.

All support will be disclosed. Partners may correct factual errors but cannot buy favorable conclusions or suppress negative findings. See the [editorial policy](EDITORIAL_POLICY.md). Outreach history, contact research, drafts and agreements remain private operational material.
