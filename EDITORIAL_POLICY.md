# Editorial independence and transparency

Mini PC AI Lab is an independent initiative in its initial development stage. Evaluation hardware does not purchase favorable conclusions. Negative findings will be retained, and manufacturers cannot control technical conclusions or suppress publication of valid limitations.

Every result must disclose donated, loaned, discounted, or purchased hardware; financial relationships; affiliate relationships if any; and who owns the equipment. Record return dates, shipping costs, damage responsibilities, data wiping, and other obligations before accepting a loan. Agreements require explicit owner approval.

Manufacturers may review factual specifications and identify reproducible errors. They do not receive editorial approval rights. Corrections must include the date, what changed, why, and whether conclusions changed. Preserve prior result provenance where possible without retaining private information.

Measurements, estimates, manufacturer specifications, reported outcomes, and proposals must be distinguishable. Publish errors and excluded trials with reasons. Missing telemetry is unknown, never zero. Disclose workload scope, model and software versions, funding, and limits of comparison.

No audience size, review history, coverage guarantee, sponsorship, or performance result may be invented. Public materials and screenshots require owner review. Private project evidence is summarized with sanitized references; raw proprietary code and commercial assets remain private.
